{# How it works #}

How It Works

From onboarding to compliance in four simple steps.

1

Register or Sign In

Caregivers self-register. Admins create manager accounts and manage the full team.

2

Schedule Visits

Admins assign caregivers to clients, setting dates, times, and any special care notes.

3

Caregivers Check In

At the client's home, caregivers check in via the dashboard — optionally with GPS location.

4

Review Compliance

Managers and admins review real-time compliance reports and act on any alerts.
